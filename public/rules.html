Event Rules & Clarifications

Read carefully before participating

Tile Clarifications
Yama
You MUST receive all of the items as an actual drop. You CANNOT craft them. You CANNOT use any contracts to speed up the drop process either.
All Multi-Drop Tiles
All tiles that have multiples will count for the next tier. For example, in Zulrah — Tier 1 requires 1 unique, Tier 2 requires 2 total uniques, etc.
Kalphite Queen
All drops for this tile MUST come from KQ, and you must show the looting log to prove it.
Pets
Any pets obtained during the event will award the team that got them 5 BONUS POINTS at the end of the event.
Exceptions: Any pet that is NOT from a boss or activity on the board will not count. Rocky will count from its respective activities only. Beef will NOT give bonuses on top of its tile requirement.
Raids
Raids uniques must be earned in your team's name for it to count toward your board.
Bingo Scoring
+5 pts
Per Completed Row, Column, or Diagonal
+5 pts
Per Pet Obtained
From eligible bosses/activities on the board only
Blackout
Automatic Win
First team to complete every tile wins outright — regardless of points or pet bonuses
General Rules
  1. 1. Follow all Rules and Guidelines.
  2. 2. Any form of discrimination, toxicity, or exclusion will not be tolerated.
  3. 3. You MUST have your Clan Events plugin turned on and it must be included in all screenshots/submissions.
  4. 4. You MUST have the XP Updater Plugin with WiseOldMan turned on.
  5. 5. Any drops, XP gains, etc., must be completed by you on any accounts you play on. These accounts MUST be specified in your sign-up.
  6. 6. You may use other accounts to help your main one, however if the other accounts get a drop, it will not count.
  7. 6a. If you are swapping accounts, it MUST be specified in the clock-in and clock-out channel.
  8. 7. Any cheating will not be tolerated and action will be taken immediately.
  9. 8. You may not attempt to grief anyone on your team or the opposite team — whether that means trying to kill one another, giving inexperienced players wrong advice intentionally, etc.
  10. 9. All submissions MUST be posted in the designated submissions channels. They also need to include the event password as stated in Rule #3.
  11. 10. You are encouraged to post your submissions as soon as the tiles are completed. Holding submissions is not allowed.
  12. 11. No pre-stacking! Use your stacked rewards at the respective events.
